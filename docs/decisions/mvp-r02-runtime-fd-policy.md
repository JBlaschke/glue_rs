# Initial raw runtime FD interval policy

Status: accepted for bounded source implementation; native admission remains open.

Add a fixed `evaluate_runtime_fd_policy_raw` factory over the original runtime recipe evidence and complete raw program/inventory/errors/event iterable. Invoke the recipe reader once. Preserve all original phases and entry attempts; accept no caller-created capture, normalized operation, authority boolean or policy selector. Keep existing synthetic state/mapping engines and public raw profiles unchanged.

Handle the existing eleven scalar FD names (close/cancel, dup, dup2, write/cancel, pwrite/cancel, fsync/cancel and ftruncate) plus ordinary open/open_nocancel/openat/openat_nocancel request intent through the pinned existing classifier. Canonical writable/create/truncate/append opens and known path mutation attempts remain forbidden even on syscall error. Unhandled fcntl, reads/queries, vectors, mapping, asynchronous, cwd, opaque and unknown routes make the whole result incomplete. Expand no legacy161-call declaration table in this feature.

Compute capture-local slot versions and stable regions outside closed possible lookup/effect windows. Propagation needs stable source/destination windows; overlapping or touching relevant intervals, unknown producers and allocation reservation hazards cannot be ordered by return. A postcondition after a complete nonconflicting interval does not place the effect at return. Failed close poisons its slot; failed dup2 poisons its target; failed writes retain partial-effect uncertainty and their full attempted request. Reuse and change-and-restore never recover an earlier version.

Retain READY output observations only as initial provenance. Every exec, including the first, retires ALL output binding certainty: clear CLOEXEC does not exclude MAC closure or internal stdio repair. Post-exec stdout/stderr writes remain unresolved and incomplete until a separately reviewed post-exec witness exists. Do not fabricate an output exemption or kernel/OFD generation.

Whole validated physical captures that fail image/Mach/process interpretation may expose all original entry attempts in an explicitly incomplete result, with no state/exemption prefix. Invalid evidence/transport returns no successful policy result. Partition every original phase into its validated bootstrap, pair, exec/EJUSTRETURN, termination, lifecycle or unsupported role without inventing successful nonreturning calls.

All native, output-exemption authority, state-feed eligibility, successful-effect-linearization and full-trace-acceptance flags remain false. Source-conditional clean means the finite request policy closed; it is not macOS no-extraction acceptance. Focused original-byte controls must exercise interval hazards, failures, reuse, exec retirement, unsupported/late input and immutable serialization on both supported Python versions.
